Role-Based Capabilities

Decoupled operational boundaries for different user types.

Requesters Portal

Students and faculty can report repairs, select categories (Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC), map locations, monitor status updates, and verify/close resolved work orders.

Technician Workbench

Maintenance staff receive specific ticket allocations, mark assignments as active (In Progress), and submit descriptive resolution notes to resolve issues.

Admin Console

Facilities managers can monitor overall campus workloads, adjust prioritization, assign staff members to submitted tickets, and manage active users.
