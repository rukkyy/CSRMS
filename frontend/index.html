Engineered Capabilities

Powerful modules. Built to scale.

Designed with modern visual aesthetics and strict boundaries for reliable campus facilities coordination.

Decoupled Workspaces

Personalized dashboards for students to log repairs, maintenance staff to manage daily tasks, and administrators to oversee assignments, set priorities, and monitor compliance metrics.

Relational Engine

Allocate Staff

Close Out
